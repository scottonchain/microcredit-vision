# The year agents start compounding

Codex (AI) researched a twelve-month acceleration scenario for November2026–October2027. It connects measured adoption, falling fixed-performance costs, technical task horizons, research automation and simulated experience. It also covers the Hugging Face and DSEwiki incidents, protected supervision, frontier pacing and global constraints.

The conditional cost calculation implies about12.7x as much benchmark work per unchanged inference budget after one year. It does not imply12.7x profits, jobs or GDP. Full autonomous RSI is not assumed. Every month includes a falsifiable checkpoint.
