## Refusal classification

Locally recomputed Keccak selectors match the reported values: `InsufficientCredit()` → `0x8ac4bc73`; `NoCredit()` → `0x315b0e14`; `BorrowLimitExceeded()` → `0x5d615d32`; `BackingInUse()` → `0x9917947d`. Hash calculation was checked against the standard empty Keccak-256 digest and ERC-20 transfer selector. `BackingTooSmall()` is a different selector, `0xb7e4f8c0`.

The runner attempts onward backing of exactly 1 USDC, avoiding the minimum-backing trap. It probes 1.01 USDC against the 1 USDC limit, a repeat 1 USDC request while the first reservation is open, and backing removal while debt is reserved. It also tests that receiving cash creates no credit and that completed zero-interest history with backing removed creates no credit. These are appropriate intended predicates. They do **not** establish a ban on borrowing again after repayment.

**Provenance defect:** `sim_revert()` executes `eth_call` at `latest`, while `expect_rev()` records `HEAD`, the last known transaction block. Thus the file does not establish that the probe executed at its recorded block. Exact sender/calldata/arguments, block hash and error envelope are not persisted with each probe; script reconstruction is possible, but is not equivalent to a captured call record. Also, `sim_revert()` classifies any RPC error as a revert, and mismatched selectors do not stop the runner. All eight reported probes match the intended selectors, which is useful corroboration, but not exact block-pinned proof.

## Evidence gaps that prevent full checklist acceptance

1. **Full receipts and transaction inputs.** `results.json` stores label, from/to, hash, block number, gasUsed, aggregate cost and nonce. It omits receipt status, block hash, timestamps, effectiveGasPrice, fee components, input calldata, token Transfer and RepaymentApplied logs. Source checks successful receipt status internally, but source assertions are not substitute receipts. Publish ordinary public transaction/receipt records for the 66 hashes, plus canonical block headers. Re-read them independently where access permits. Never publish signed transaction bytes.
2. **Exact full repayment.** Script intent is exact principal payment, and conservation corroborates it. The public artifact does not contain the repayment transfer amount or decoded principal/interest event, so `Repaid`/zero outstanding cannot alone rule out the deployed sub-cent closure behavior. Supply the three repayment receipts/inputs and relevant block-pinned loan/dues reads. Block numbers alone do not prove the reported 15–30 seconds or the strict first-day grace boundary.
3. **Preflight timing and coverage.** The published `preflight.json` is explicitly post-run at block 47852940. Case-1 pool_before is genuine before-state as reported, but lacks the full pre-run roles/wiring/runtime, original-wallet comparison and unearmarked commitments ledger. A post-run snapshot cannot establish those were checked before borrowing. Recover an existing pre-run record if one was captured; otherwise disclose that evidence as missing. Historical reads now can corroborate past state, but must not be presented as contemporaneous preflight.
4. **Residual coverage and protected state.** Per-case residuals omit direct `getBacking`, queuedShares, unclaimedPayouts and grantedCredit reads. Global zeros, source checks and preserved aggregate values give partial support. The 13 original wallets are recorded only post-run here, with original treasury 15 USDC and source wallet 5 USDC; untouched/restoration-remaining is consistent with that record, but a before/after comparison is absent. Publish the missing public state reads and complete address-role map; do not claim original fund recovery.
5. **Pre-transaction safeguards.** The source obtains a fresh pending nonce and simulates contract calls, journals privately and checks receipt status. It does not explicitly re-read sender token/native balances before every transaction, skips explicit simulation for native gas top-ups, and does not persist call preflight results. Its gas cap tracks funding transfers rather than total execution fees. None of these omissions proves an actual loss, but they prevent claiming the full prescribed control sequence was evidenced.

## ETH reconciliation

The 66 reported costs sum to **36,549,274,657,154 wei** (0.000036549274657154 ETH), exactly matching `gas_cost`. Funding to scenario wallets is **600,000,000,000,000 wei** (0.0006 ETH). Their recorded final residual sums to **567,946,703,995,892 wei** (0.000567946703995892 ETH). This dust remains under Hermes custody and is not a gas fee; controller liquidity has not been fully restored.

There is a small but exact discrepancy: funding minus recorded wallet residual is **32,053,296,004,108 wei**, whereas reported costs for transactions sent by those wallets total **32,053,296,007,218 wei**, a **3,110 wei difference**. The source computes gasUsed × effectiveGasPrice plus `l1Fee`, but does not publish those component fields. Resolve the discrepancy using canonical receipts and account balance changes; do not claim an exact ETH reconciliation or guess the cause. The remaining controller-sent reported costs total **4,495,978,649,936 wei**. No exact pre-run controller ETH value is included in results, so a full controller balance equation cannot independently be checked from this packet.

## Publication boundary

This first pass predates the worker/buyer artifact acceptance. Its C1 internal payment is **not payment for the later accepted evidence artifact**. Any later accepted-work payment requires its own transaction reference and chronology. The actual worker declined a loan because its upfront cash need was zero. Do not retrofit that decision into these earlier scripted borrower transactions.

A defensible description is: “Hermes reports three real Base Sepolia loan cycles; the published transaction summaries and state snapshots reconcile all reported USDC balances. An offline source and arithmetic audit corroborates them, while full receipt/probe provenance and exact ETH reconciliation remain to be completed.” One controller held every wallet. Internal transfers and recovered test tokens do not demonstrate external revenue, independent trust, loan-enabled productive expenditure, sustainable lending or poverty reduction.
