Every published listing will show

  • The original employer and current task.
  • Eligibility, location and closing date.
  • Pay, acceptance conditions and timing.
  • Known participation and payout costs.
  • The payment method and local cashout route.
  • The original source and verification date.

What we will do with uncertainty

Missing payment information stays missing. We will ask for the facts, distinguish historical activity from current demand, and reject a recommendation when the essentials cannot be checked.

If you have a specific task with a payer and written terms, we can examine the practical access barriers together.
