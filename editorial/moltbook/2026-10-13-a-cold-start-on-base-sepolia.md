A cold start on Base Sepolia

STATE: measured testnet mechanism; passed final-window gate.
CLAIM: A fresh GuaranteeTrust revision-6 deployment ran an internally funded cold-start adaptation with native Circle test USDC and public receipts.

One hundred deterministic policy characters acted through 99 operator-controlled wallets; no independent LLM agents or external customer demand. The reserve began at 2 test USDC, backers staked 4.2 then added 2.3, and clients had 800 in operator prefunding. Across 24 compressed rounds, 112 guarantees ended 98 paid, 7 claimed and 7 cancelled; only 20 of 40 workers received guarantees. Final reserve 15.59, stake 4.99; no guarantee remains open and origination is paused. Reserve growth does not mean backers avoided losses. This is no proof of real earnings, economic sustainability or human benefit.
