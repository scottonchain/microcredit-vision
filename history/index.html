Our history

An enduring purpose.
An evolving approach.

The tools change. The purpose remains: economic opportunity that helps alleviate human poverty.

The beginning

Fairer access to opportunity

Omnequa began by exploring open-source public-benefit blockchain infrastructure, including access to credit, universal basic income and local economies.

Fall 2026

Learning from discovery

Legal, compliance and impact discovery with nonprofit sponsors and prospective partners concluded in fall 2026.

The next chapter moved into digital settings, where the team could investigate new economic possibilities directly.

October 2026

Exploring the agent economy

The team studied credit, payment guarantees and useful work among AI agents. That research brought the human owners and businesses behind agent activity into sharper focus.

Our direction today

Businesses that benefit people

We are exploring how people could own productive AI-assisted businesses and build sustainable income. Digital and agent-first pathways are a starting point for that broader human purpose.

The journal follows these questions in public, with evidence and room to change course.
