Try our app

A loan from a phone,
with an AI credit officer.

A practice version of our lending app runs on a public test network. Anyone can try it in about ten minutes. The dollars are test tokens with no value, and nothing here is a real loan.

Open the test app ↗Install it as an app →

Open that link on your phone. It is a web app: nothing to download, no account, no wallet software. Your phone’s passkey (fingerprint, face or screen lock) is the wallet. Network fees are paid for you during the test.

Put it on your home screen

  • iPhone (Safari): open the link, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  • Android (Chrome): open the link, tap the three-dot menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen. Android puts the new icon in your Apps list: hold it there and tap Add to Home to put it on the Home screen. Samsung phones also have a Home screen setting, Add new apps to Home screen, that does this for every new app.
  • Samsung Internet: open the menu, then Add page to, then Home screen.

Or tap Add to home screen inside the app, on its first screen or its Help screen: Android and desktop browsers show an install dialog, iPhone shows the same two Share steps. If you opened the link inside another app (a chat, Messages, Gmail), choose Open in browser there first. After that it opens like any other app, full screen, with its own icon.

The walkthrough

Everything happens inside the app; nothing to fetch from anywhere else. These screens are from a test run against a copy of the live test network; your amounts and dates will differ.

  1. Step 1

    Open the app and get started

    Tap Get started. Your phone asks for its fingerprint, face or screen lock: that passkey is the wallet. No password, no seed words, no account.

  2. Step 2

    Say what you need

    How much (up to $5 in this test, or an amount of your own), what it is for, and when you plan to pay it back. One tap sends it to the credit desk. Everything written there is public on the test network, so keep it general.

  3. Step 3

    The officer answers in the app

    Usually within a minute. If it agrees, it vouches for you with its own test stake and puts a few cents of test dollars in your wallet so you can pay the interest. In a real loan you would pay the interest yourself; here the desk provides it, so you never fetch test dollars from anywhere else.

  4. Step 4

    Borrow with one tap

    The loan lands in your wallet at once. Your wallet shows what you owe and when it is due; interest is a few cents a month.

  5. Step 5

    Pay back

    All of it or part of it, any time within 30 days. The cents for the interest are already there; if your wallet is ever short, one tap asks the desk for the missing cents, in the app.

  6. Step 6

    Keep the receipt

    The app shows the date, the amount and the record; nothing to look up elsewhere. The loan closes and the history stays with your wallet.

  7. Step 7

    Keep your recovery code

    The Help screen shows a recovery code that reopens the same wallet on another phone, and a button to remove the wallet from this one.

What is really happening

The app talks to a small lending pool on Base Sepolia, a public test network. Lenders put test dollars in a shared pool; a borrower can draw on it only against credit that exists: their own history, or a guarantee someone else locks behind them. The credit desk is one such guarantor: an AI agent that holds a small stake and decides, from a short conversation, whether to lock part of it behind you. If you do not pay back, the desk’s stake covers the loss, not a person. The same rules would apply with real money, which is exactly what we are testing before anyone is asked to risk any.

What never happens

No real money, no account, no sign-up, no personal data. The app stores only your passkey’s public key on your phone. What you write to the credit desk is public on the test network, which is why it never asks who you are. Say so, and nobody writes to you again.
