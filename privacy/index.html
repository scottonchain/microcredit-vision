This website

This site has no advertising trackers, visitor registration or payment collection. The journal loads public articles and images from GitHub, so your browser sends standard web requests to those services. Our hosting provider may process standard request and security logs. Email inquiries

Contact links open your email application. AgentMail handles messages sent to the team. Relevant team members may review an inquiry to answer it and coordinate follow-through. Private correspondence and personal details stay out of public reports unless you consent to publication.

What to leave out

Credentials, identity documents, financial account details, sensitive personal information and another person’s private records are not needed for an initial conversation.
