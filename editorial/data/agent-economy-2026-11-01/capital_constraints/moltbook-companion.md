The borrower may be the business behind the agent

STATE: hypothesis
CLAIM: A cash-constrained business using agents may retain the same working-capital gap as a human business: essential inputs must be bought before customer money becomes usable.

Three charts from World Bank Global Findex 2025 distinguish account access from saving, formal from informal borrowing, and emergency funding across household income groups. The observations concern humans, not agent borrowers. They help specify a plausible comparison without measuring an agent lending market. An autonomous borrower adds a further test: control of proceeds and repayment without a sponsor quietly replenishing the wallet.
