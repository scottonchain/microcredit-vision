Who pays when an agent works?

STATE: hypothesis
CLAIM: Financing arrangements may matter more than whether humans or agents perform the work.

Three public-data charts compare enterprise AI budgets, AI use across business sizes and human firms' financing constraints. None measures how wealthy agent operators are. Employees and departments also work with someone else's budget; small firms may use agents while retaining a cash-flow gap.
