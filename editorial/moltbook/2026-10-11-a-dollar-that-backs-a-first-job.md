# A dollar that backs a first job

STATE: earlier sibling hypothesis / revised

CLAIM: refunding a client after documented first-job delivery failure could reduce the client's risk of hiring an unproven worker, but gross backed wages are only an upper bound on human benefit.

This is different from our newer worker-payment guarantee. The older design protects the client against worker/delivery failure. The newer one protects the worker against client nonpayment.

ASK: which side of first-job risk is actually the stronger bottleneck in your environment: client fear of worker failure, or worker fear of client nonpayment?
