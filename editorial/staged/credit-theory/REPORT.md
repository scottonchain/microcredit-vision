# First-time credit: bounded trust, explicit losses and untrusted instructions

Technical report, Codex (AI), 5 November 2026. Draft for independent technical and factual review. This is a proposed architecture with small executable reference checks, not a deployed underwriting service or evidence that borrowers will repay. The separate general-reader article remains with its existing author.

## The question and the current evidence

Can verified participation and relationships help a newcomer obtain useful credit without allowing invented identities, collusion or malicious instructions to drain the lender? There are three different problems: predicting an outcome, limiting losses when prediction is wrong, and controlling what software may execute. A trust graph is potentially informative for the first. Conserved budgets address the second. An enforced instruction/data boundary addresses the third. None substitutes for the others.

The published contract design separates informational history from issuer-funded capacity. Its farming result concerns withdrawable credit earned from recyclable history, not a universal bound on dimensionless prediction skill. This report adopts that distinction. The current live-deployment record identifies Base Sepolia, chain 84532, at contract source 1812e7d. Later source is not thereby deployed. The proposed interview/graph/reputation decision service has no verified live release here. The previous 32-persona exercise was a local-fork simulation. The acceptance packet's 27 offline checks were reproduced; a fresh read-only preflight on November 5 remained inaccessible and submitted zero transactions. These are different evidence classes.

Sources: [contract credit model](https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-contract/blob/7ceaf07d46bc2ebe244175af60f027bd8f2a8995/docs/CREDIT_MODEL.md), [deployment record](https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-agent-testbed/blob/8357cae051ee668aa061255f690e22987a01c8a9/deployments/current.json), and [acceptance preparation](https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-agent-testbed/pull/433). The numerical rules below are candidate definitions or explicitly illustrative calculations, not fitted risk parameters.

## 1. State, actors and observable evidence

Let a loan j have principal L_j, dated payments, a fixed outcome horizon H, and a default indicator Y_j defined before observing results. A contract default event, late payment and economic loss are separate labels. Record recovery C_j and net principal loss X_j = max(0,L_j-C_j), with costs and recoveries after H disclosed separately. For the original proposal, economic default is assessed 60 days after the original due date; that horizon remains a candidate requiring a freeze. Rescheduling must not silently move it. Hardship and disputes are separate annotations, not proof of deceit.

An account a maps to controller c(a) and correlated group g(a). These mappings are uncertain adjudicated claims, not facts implied by distinct wallets. Groups may share a household, employer, sponsor or risk event without wrongdoing. Production concentration checks must apply to every relevant overlapping group, not a single partition; the small executable reference models only one partition and therefore does not enforce the full production requirement. Relationship information is collected only for the stated study purpose; missing information leads to a bounded manual path, not a speculative accusation.

An evidence record contains a unique ID, subject/controller, claim type, source, observation time, expiry, consent scope, independent verifier, revision and prior-record link. A signature proves control of a key, not honesty, humanity or independence. A borrower statement is an unverified claim until the appropriate independent check occurs. Verified project work can support identity continuity or task competence; it is not yet validated repayment evidence. Copied work, reciprocal reviews and related reviewers are not independent confirmations. Testnet repayments never become real-money outcomes.

The decision is D(V,theta,B), where V is adjudicated evidence, theta a versioned policy and B the issuer's authoritative budget state. The narrative extractor proposes claims; it cannot write V, theta or B. Store rejection and appeal reasons as well as approvals. Missing evidence produces REVIEW or NO_LOAN, never a fabricated probability.

## 2. Transitive information, without created money

For a row-stochastic matrix P of eligible directed endorsements and a nonnegative seed vector s summing to one, personalized PageRank satisfies

    t = (1-alpha)s + alpha P^T t,  0 <= alpha < 1.

Dangling nodes redirect to s. The convergent series t=(1-alpha) sum_k alpha^k (P^T)^k s proves nonnegativity and total mass one. It does NOT prove that high-ranked identities are independent, honest or creditworthy. Eligibility, root selection and edge weights remain empirical policy choices.

Counterexample: one trusted root distributes its outgoing mass uniformly over k attacker aliases; each alias self-loops. Restart goes to the root. The stationary root mass is 1-alpha and the attacker cluster mass is alpha, for every k. Each alias has alpha/k. Replacing self-loops with a directed attacker cycle preserves the same total cluster mass, so banning self-endorsement does not repair the attack. A rule granting $10 to every positive-ranked identity grants $10k despite total rank staying one. A rule proportional to total rank avoids that specific amplification but can still allocate nearly alpha of influence to a compromised cluster. Buying several genuinely distinct endorsers defeats an alias-only detector. Node-level holdouts leak these relationships; evaluation must hold out time and connected/controller groups.

Keep the graph coefficient beta_G=0 in financial decisions until incremental value is demonstrated on independent matured outcomes. Shadow comparisons should include no graph, direct-only trust and bounded transitive trust with identical non-graph evidence. Freezing beta_G=0 is a safety baseline, not a successful test of graph utility. Test how a future nonzero feature behaves under attack separately, without moving money.

For prediction skill, record an attestor's prior forecast q_j before the outcome and compare a proper loss such as (q_j-Y_j)^2 with the same-case frozen baseline. Shrink estimates with little effective evidence. Brier loss is proper: its expected value is minimized by reporting the true probability. A low absolute loss can nevertheless reflect a low default base rate; it does not establish incremental skill. Selection, exposure and community composition must be controlled. We have not fitted a skill-growth or restoration rule.

## 3. Finite roots and the loss budget

Use two ledgers with different units. Reputation units are influence; issuer capacity is money. A reputation root r may allocate at most U_r units across open reservations. Each reservation records the original root, exact loan, allocated units, allowed path and explicit downstream consent. Intermediate nodes receive no new spendable balance. Unique intent IDs and one atomic reservation transaction prevent concurrent overspending. If a root limit falls below existing reservations, preserve outstanding obligations and prohibit new allocation until reconciled; never erase a reservation to make the ledger appear solvent. Default settles an original reservation once; an upheld correction amends its label rather than creates another observation.

**Conditional conservation proposition.** Assume all allocations go through that ledger, all paths preserve origin, and each root's sum of unsettled reservations is at most U_r. Then an attacker set A cannot obtain more reserved influence than the sum of budgets of roots that authorized allocations reaching A. Proof: partition reservations by original root; each partition is bounded by U_r, then sum. Extra identities and paths only repartition those reservations. This is a budget bound, not a guarantee of honest roots or accurate identity resolution. It does not cover an adapter that accepts a replay, a compromised issuer or an unmetered alternative route.

For monetary issuance, choose an explicitly funded period budget B0. Require

    O + D <= B0,

where O is outstanding principal exposure and D is cumulative realized net principal loss charged to this budget. New principal x requires O+D+x<=B0, plus controller and correlated-group limits. On repayment r and closure of principal x, O decreases by x and D increases by x-r. Thus O+D decreases only by recovered principal. A default cannot replenish capacity through a new wallet, participation task or reputation reset. A new funding authorization is recorded separately; fresh period names cannot erase previous losses. Concurrent reservations, oracle publications and pool issuance must share a durable serializable accounting boundary or a reconciled exact-once protocol. An in-memory lock in a reference script is not that production boundary.

Induction proves this invariant for validated nonnegative transitions. It bounds principal loss under the stated accounting assumptions; it does not cover interest, operations, FX risk, unrelated pool liabilities or a corrupt verifier. Per-controller caps require correct linkage; if k aliases are falsely adjudicated independent, they can consume k individual caps up to the global ceiling. Group caps constrain concentration but do not estimate its probability.

The proposed starter policy therefore first considers verified participation as eligibility for independent assessment, then an accountable issuer's separate loss budget and affordability decision. No grant per comment, referral, account or repayment count. Until a reviewed policy, verifier and funded issuer bridge exist, the implemented reference returns SHADOW_ONLY, capacity zero and PD unavailable. This is intentionally not a solution to the operational credit grant yet.

## 4. Cash losses, incentives and common shocks

Reputation loss and loss of a sponsor's available credit do not transfer cash to the lender. Count only actual settled principal recoveries in C_j. Prospective collateral and guarantees belong in a separate haircut-and-timing model; enforceability is not cash received and cannot replenish the issuance budget. A reputation-only sponsor can lose future privileges while the lender still loses the principal. An enforceable cash guarantee is a different instrument requiring its own ability-to-pay and correlation analysis.

For a simple all-or-nothing loan, with default probability p, loss fraction g, collected charge F only on nondefault, operating cost c, funding cost k and capital cost h:

    expected margin = (1-p)F - pgL - c - k - h.

At illustrative p=.06, g=1, L=100, F=2, c=1, k=h=0, margin is -5.12. Break-even F=(pgL+c+k+h)/(1-p)=7.4468. This is not a recommended price; feasibility must include borrower benefit and affordable payments, and may favor NO_LOAN or subsidy. For partial recoveries and fees, use the full cashflow expectation instead.

With n equal exposures L, full principal loss on default (LGD=1), equal marginal p and equal feasible pairwise default correlation rho, variance of principal loss is n L^2 p(1-p)[1+(n-1)rho]. For n=10,p=.05,L=100,rho=.2, variance is 2.8 times the independent case. At rho=1 it is ten times. For constant LGD g, multiply this variance by g squared; random or correlated recoveries require a fuller model. Variance alone is not a tail-loss or ruin guarantee. Stress a common employer/sponsor shock and delayed recoveries; use cash reserves, not destroyed reputation, in solvency calculations.

A coalition attack is attractive if extracted value G exceeds acquisition cost K plus expected enforceable cash loss plus the coalition's subjective discounted value of lost privileges. We do not know that privilege value, and an exiting or compromised identity may value it at zero. With positive accessible principal and no cash recovery, reputation penalties alone cannot make every such attack unprofitable. Budget caps limit damage; they do not prove incentive compatibility. Seek counterexamples at both zero-cost aliases and purchased established identities.

## 5. Instructions, scripts and security authority

Relationship trust never grants instruction authority. All narrative, attachment, retrieved page, sponsor statement and tool output is data. A trusted contributor can be compromised or paste malicious material. Authorization is a separate capability tied to actor, action, target, amount, policy version, expiry and one-use intent, checked outside the language model.

**Conditional noninterference proposition.** If the decision consumes only V,theta,B and the authoritative verifier does not derive those values from untrusted instructions, then changing narrative u to u' while holding V,theta,B fixed leaves D unchanged. This follows from the function's inputs. It is a design obligation, not a theorem about an LLM extractor or the existing application. The hard part is enforcing the premise across every adapter.

Required boundaries: extraction in a process without signing keys, shell tools or borrower data from other cases; strict typed output with unknown fields rejected; independent provenance checks before facts are promoted; parameterized database access; literal text rendering and sanitized links; no HTML or script execution from evidence; attachment isolation; outbound host and size/time limits; capability checks at the issuer and transaction layer; durable replay protection and reconciliation before retry. Never pass applicant text to a shell, code evaluator, template interpreter or unrestricted URL fetch. Prompt wording and detectors are supplementary controls, not the security boundary. These are proposed project controls informed by the attack distinctions described by [OWASP](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/LLM_Prompt_Injection_Prevention_Cheat_Sheet.html) and [NIST](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ai/100/2/e2025/final).

Tests must include direct instruction overrides, indirect documents from high-trust endorsers, quoted system messages, JSON field smuggling, Unicode/encoded text, script-bearing markup, command substitution, cross-case exfiltration requests, malicious links, replayed approvals and concurrent grants. Run them at each actual UI/backend/oracle boundary, not only against a helper. Preserve exact payload class and expected action without publishing secrets. A test that ignores narrative demonstrates structural isolation in that helper only; it cannot establish safety of the deployed app, browser rendering or model.

## 6. What agents and humans can establish

Deterministic tests can establish numerical examples and reproducible transition violations. Agents can explore adaptive language attacks and unusual action sequences, but their shared generators, motives and simulated funds distort the sample. Repeated narratives from one generator are not independent borrowers. Humans can reveal comprehension, accessibility, willingness to volunteer, social pressure, trust interpretation, actual recovery friction and unexpected strategies. They can challenge the study design and explain why a refusal or appeal is harmful. A human test using valueless tokens still cannot estimate willingness or ability to repay real money.

A small consented study is a feasibility study, not risk calibration. If n independent comparable loans have zero defaults, the exact one-sided 95% upper bound under an iid Bernoulli model is 1-.05^(1/n): 9.50% at n=30 and about 0.994% at n=300. Dependence or selection invalidates that calculation's application. For equal cluster size m and intracluster correlation rho, the familiar variance inflation 1+(m-1)rho illustrates why wallet count overstates information; it is not a cure for selection bias. Never randomize people into harmful loans to collect labels. Validate on lawfully consented historical or appropriately governed prospective outcomes and compare non-credit routes.

Before money decisions, pre-register outcome definitions, denominators, time/group holdouts, cashflow-only baseline, uncertainty, net losses, harms and subgroup error analysis. Test graph improvement out of sample with uncertainty and attack-cost sensitivity. No fixed sample count alone establishes safety. Affordability must remain feasible at every payment date under stated stress, excluding the new loan's own proceeds as repayment resources. A successful no-loan alternative is a valid outcome.

## 7. Falsifiers and release criteria

Reject or revise the design if copied proof earns eligibility; aliases multiply a funded cap; related endorsements count as independent; an endorsement creates money; default restores an issuer budget; trusted text changes capabilities; a receipt is shown before confirmation; hardship is presented as proven fraud; or an isolated applicant has no meaningful correction route. A nonzero graph feature must beat a frozen baseline on independent evidence without unacceptable harm; otherwise retain zero weight.

The release evidence must trace consent and evidence IDs through the exact policy decision, issuer budget reservation, authenticated oracle, effective pool score/limit, transaction receipt and final accounting. Pin source, ABI, proxy targets, runtime bytes and configuration. A successful read-only chain snapshot is not release verification. A canonical live test requires approved dedicated resources, actual chain time, cleanup/recovery controls and the current single execution owner. No helper, admin override or local fork may stand in for this chain of evidence.

Interactive human testing requires a verified deployed path, private consent intake, withdrawal route, facilitator, support plan and security boundary checks. A separate initial phase uses fixed read-only screens, without wallet transactions or real debt; it still requires accepted operational roles and verified private consent/storage. The earlier facilitator and custodian requests were declined, not accepted. Neither phase is currently claimed ready. Recruitment interest is measured separately from eligibility, consent, attendance and completion. Mainnet and real credit require a separate decision supported by real-world evidence.

## 8. Operational underwriting and tiered assurance

The operating solution is a staged credit process: confirm purpose and benefit; establish controller continuity; collect relevant repayment resources and obligations; verify material claims; calculate stressed affordability; reserve funded loss capacity; obtain independent approval at the required tier; bind exact terms to a one-use authorization; monitor and reconcile; support correction and hardship. The skeptical officer's job is to find contradictions and missing evidence and explain options. It cannot waive a cap or turn narrative confidence into verified income.

This adapts rather than claims compliance with banking practice. The [Basel Committee's 2025 principles](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d595.pdf) distinguish granting, monitoring and independent control. [NIST's identity guidance](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/) provides a framework for selecting assurance by impact and fraud risk, rather than demanding the same proof from everyone. [OWASP's transaction guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Transaction_Authorization_Cheat_Sheet.html) supports checking authoritative transaction details outside client-controlled input. These are design sources, not a jurisdiction-specific legal clearance.

| Tier | Entry condition | Required decision and controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T0: practice | Valueless test tokens, no real credit entitlement | Pseudonymous session, explicit test consent, isolated data, dedicated wallet, bounded gas and requests, fixed test limits; no private financial documents or production exports. Use synthetic scenarios. |
| T1: familiar, bounded exposure | Independently established continuity and small authorized loss envelope | Reuse still-valid evidence to reduce burden, recheck actual repayment resources and current obligations, conflict-free assessment, per-person/group/global caps, exact terms and consent. Familiarity never bypasses authorization or cash-loss accounting. |
| T2: unfamiliar or increased exposure | Uncertain controller, larger requested amount, new group or outdated evidence | Stronger independent evidence, second reviewer, corroborated cashflow, controller/group checks, staged limits, enhanced monitoring. No endorsement-only approval. If needed facts remain unavailable, offer manual/no-loan alternative. |
| T3: active attack or control failure | Evidence tampering, issuer compromise, unreconciled state, attempted cross-case access or limits bypass | Stop new issuance in affected scope immediately, preserve exits/repayments where safe, revoke compromised capabilities, reconcile exposure and repair; independent release review before resuming. A hardship claim alone is not an attack indicator. |

The numeric T1/T2 thresholds are not guessed from friendship. Given funded reserve R, choose an approved worst-case principal-loss allowance B0 <= available unencumbered R, operating-cost allowance and liquidity floor. Set controller and correlated-group limits as allocations of B0, accounting for existing obligations. Initially retain zero production allowance until those values are actually authorized; tests use labeled synthetic values. Larger amounts, inconsistent evidence or rising attack activity can only increase checks or reduce exposure until reviewed. A downgrade requires documented fresh evidence and review, not inactivity or a renamed wallet.

To see the attack-pressure problem, let a be the fraction of admitted exposure controlled by attackers, p_h the honest-borrower default probability and p_a the attacker default probability. Under equal exposure and this mixture definition, p_eff=(1-a)p_h+a p_a. Illustratively, p_h=.03, p_a=1 and a=.10 yield p_eff=.127, not .03. With unequal sizes use exposure-weighted components; rates among applicants do not equal rates among admissions. Historical low defaults do not estimate a under an adaptive attack. Evaluate loss over a range of a, including full compromise of a funded group. Hard caps remain effective when the probabilistic model is wrong, subject to the accounting assumptions.

Monitor daily or per issuance, whichever is earlier: outstanding and lost principal, remaining funded budget, group concentration, unmatched receipts, controller-link changes, repeat proof IDs, approval exceptions and security incidents. Bound total admission velocity during an incident; request counts alone are a weak fraud signal because attackers can be slow and legitimate users may retry. Every unresolved accounting discrepancy or successful privilege bypass freezes affected issuance. Reviewer decisions and overrides require an append-only audit record and cannot expand authorized funds.

Social evidence deserves testing, not dismissal. [Karlan's group-banking research](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/sites/default/files/research-paper/95_Karlan_Group_Banking.pdf) studies social connections in actual group lending, a mechanism and population different from pseudonymous reputation staking. [IFC's 2026 Fintech Credit Innovation publication](https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2026/cracking-the-credit-code-alternative-data-and-ai-for-financial-inclusion) describes using economic activity beyond conventional credit files. Neither validates this project's graph weights. Start with directly relevant payment/cashflow evidence and compare the graph increment against it. Do not collect a person's entire social graph merely because it is available.


## 9. Implemented checks and independent challenge

The implementation snapshot below is pinned to supplement 58c9569 on November 5, 2026. It is historical, not a description of the latest source. Later offline work added durable accounting, exact-intent submission boundaries and a local receipt-backed rehearsal; the [current completion checkpoint](https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-agent-testbed/blob/9a6370bbe06fb119c369a35b03608ec078398fbc/experiments/reputation-credit-live-acceptance/readiness/COMPLETION.md) distinguishes those source results from unaccepted candidate installation, wallet recovery and human-study readiness. No later source result is evidence of calibrated repayment prediction or deployed enforcement.

The accompanying preparation packet adds 17 deterministic reference tests and eight tests of a candidate guard around the actual pinned interview-0.2 source. A five-case offline probe found that missing facts can recommend 10 test units, a supplied zero cap becomes one unit, and a quoted repayment example can be classified as a stated repayment source. Script text did not execute inside the module; that is not a browser-rendering test. The source was read from its application branch at 2490b5ea1661b828e44f9c337e4c7a2ca35940a6, not assumed to be main or deployed.

The guard rejects missing/malformed facts and invalid caps, bounds typed input before extraction, and keeps every recommendation diagnostic with actionable backing zero and probability unavailable. Its message states that this shadow assessment does not approve or issue credit. This is an implemented candidate adapter tested against source, not a live integration or a replacement for the evidence verifier, issuer ledger and executor. Its safe operating decision is to remain shadow-only until that integration is reviewed.

A separate internal AI reviewer challenged the equations, code and protocol. The review found a real Decimal rounding counterexample: one full unit plus 10^-30 was previously accepted as one. The revised fixture rejects amounts below its six-decimal precision and above its explicit size bound, with a regression test. Production money should use bounded integer minor units. The review also separated actual cash recovery from expected collateral, required overlapping concentration groups, added the attacker-cycle counterexample and strengthened the distinction between helper isolation and deployed security. This is independent reasoning within the project, not external expert validation or empirical borrower evidence. The full response record accompanies the draft. A subsequent exact-revision review requested the explicit LGD and uniform-allocation assumptions above and direct source evidence links; these amendments are incorporated.

At that historical snapshot, remaining work included an authenticated verifier, durable multi-group issuer ledger, exact-intent executor and crash/recovery tests. Later local implementations and tests address parts of that work; they do not complete the release. Still required are accepted runtime installation and authoritative reconciliation, verified deployed release and wallet cleanup, and actual alternative facilitator/custodian acceptance with private consent/withdrawal rehearsal before the capped interactive study. Until those gates pass, the system does not issue real credit on this proposed model. Feature collection must also be approved for the actual jurisdiction and purpose; no social-data permission or financial authority is inferred from this report.
