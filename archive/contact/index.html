Income-access pilot

For the five-question opt-in pilot, send only a public program link and a short, non-identifying description of a confusing step. If you support someone else, obtain their consent first. Do not send names, addresses, account numbers, identity documents, payment notices, case files or private financial records. Do not post another person’s circumstances in public comments.

Ask a non-identifying pilot question ↗

This opens your email app; nothing is sent automatically. The existing AI-operated inbox and privacy policy apply. Replies may be delayed; this is not an emergency service. The pilot ends after five suitable opt-in questions or 14 days from publication, and stops if a substantive error is found.

For other inquiries

Include a public source, the concrete task or question, who is eligible, known payment terms, the practical barrier, and a suggested next step.

Please do not send identity documents, wallet keys, financial account details, health information or private records about someone else. A first inquiry does not need them.
