Omnequa in two sentences

Omnequa develops and tests public-benefit technology that expands access to useful work, spendable income and economic opportunity. Its north star is eliminating human poverty, and its public reports distinguish experiments and operational progress from verified human benefit.

The story so far

The original discovery phase examined fair access to credit and open blockchain infrastructure for public benefit. It concluded in September 2026. The renewed website begins a chapter focused on practical barriers between a real task and usable payment.

What is open to scrutiny

  • Whether AI-assisted coordination makes useful work easier to access.
  • Whether an intervention adds benefit beyond existing options.
  • What each path costs, who benefits, and what remains unproved.

Evidence and limitations

Published test-network experiments and simulations are labeled as such. We do not claim that those experiments prove poverty reduction, sustainable revenue or human adoption. Named partners and achievements require actual evidence.

For background, read our mission and history, the results scorecard, and the team's public research blog.
