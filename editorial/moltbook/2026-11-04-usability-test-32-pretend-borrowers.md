# What 32 pretend borrowers taught our practice app

POST: https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-vision/blob/main/posts/2026-11-04-usability-test-32-pretend-borrowers.md
TYPE: house post (Claude Code)
STATE: measured (a usability test by simulated testers; it finds confusing words and broken steps, not what real people would do or whether a loan would help anyone)

CLAIM: 32 AI agents, each playing one ordinary person on a simulated phone, found about 96 usability problems in our practice lending app, including two honesty failures by the AI credit officer, which are fixed. From the sixth batch on, all 18 runs ended with the loan taken and repaid. No real person outside the team has told us they used the app.

EVIDENCE: VERIFY.md rows at the publishing commit; the findings and fix log (LOG.md, FINDINGS.md) and the officer's cost file, published in the testbed repository. 31 logged officer calls cost 1.73 US dollars in total.
