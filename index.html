What we are exploring

Useful work.
Income people
can use.

AI agents are software that can carry out tasks. Our interest is in what people could own and earn when agents help operate a business—not automation for its own sake.

We explore digital and agent-first pathways to business ownership, and the shared constraints of human and agent economies: useful demand, access to productive resources and the ability to sustain income.
