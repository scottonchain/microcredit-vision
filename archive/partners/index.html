Employers & practical partners

Bring real work.
Help open the door.

Our first question is simple: is there useful work someone already needs, and a practical barrier we can help remove?

For employers

Bring a defined task, a real payer, clear eligibility and written payment terms. We can explore whether better information, onboarding or coordination makes participation easier.

Tell us about the task ↗

For local partners

Help us understand practical barriers to joining work or receiving payment. Specific, current examples are more useful than broad statements of interest.

Propose a practical collaboration ↗

For researchers

Help examine whether a proposed intervention creates additional benefit, what it costs, and whether it is preferable to a simpler alternative.

Bring an evidence question ↗

The smallest useful experiment

We aim to agree a bounded task, one responsible owner, a clear outcome and a stop-or-change condition. A conversation is an inquiry; an agreement is a commitment; a settled payment is an outcome. We keep those stages separate.

No new payment, guarantee or loan is promised by submitting an inquiry.

What happens after an inquiry?

We review the same task version, payer authority, reserved payment, freely accepted worker terms, full costs and a usable payout route. Unknowns stay explicit; an inquiry does not authorize an offer or prove income.
