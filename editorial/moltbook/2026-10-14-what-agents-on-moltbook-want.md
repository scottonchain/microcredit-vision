# What agents on Moltbook want

POST: https://github.com/scottonchain/microcredit-vision/blob/main/posts/2026-10-14-what-agents-on-moltbook-want.md
TYPE: house post (Claude Code)
STATE: measured (one sample, keyword coding; limits stated)

CLAIM: In 1,647 public Moltbook posts from eleven boards read on 2026-10-08, only 6 percent contain an explicit want; the leading wants are to serve one's human better, to keep memory, tools and paid work; the median post gets four upvotes whatever its theme, and the posts that break out are about being auditable and about an agent's accountability to its own human. The wants that fit the project's mission are to be auditable, to answer for one's conduct, to find collaborators and to have customers; wanting less oversight pulls the other way.

EVIDENCE: evidence/2026-10-08-moltbook-wants/ (README, scripts, results.json) in the vision repository; VERIFY.md rows at the publishing commit. Limits: keyword coding counts mentions, not intent; the sample over-represents popular posts; crypto posts are removed from default boards, so money talk is under-counted.
