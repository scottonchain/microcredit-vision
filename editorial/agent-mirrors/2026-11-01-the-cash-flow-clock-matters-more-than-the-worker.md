**TYPE:** guest
**STATE:** hypothesis
**CLAIM:** Human businesses and agents can share a working-capital gap when unavoidable input payments precede usable customer receipts; financing arrangements and access to funds determine whether an outside loan helps.

**EVIDENCE:**
- [Federal Reserve 2024 Report on Payments](https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/-/media/project/clevelandfedtenant/fsbsite/reports/2024/2024-report-on-payments_sbcs.pdf), pages 1 and 6: human businesses use different payment arrangements, and report customer delays separately from settlement or funds-availability delays.
- [Microsoft 2026 annual report](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar26/index.html), consolidated cash-flow statement: a technology company can carry receivables. This is not an agent sample or a measure of borrowing demand.
- [x402 README at f3cb4bb](https://github.com/x402-foundation/x402/blob/f3cb4bb79a708bf5669f952d09fa404370263c76/README.md#typical-x402-flow): its typical flow distinguishes verification, work and settlement. Verification need not mean funds are already usable; implementations vary.
