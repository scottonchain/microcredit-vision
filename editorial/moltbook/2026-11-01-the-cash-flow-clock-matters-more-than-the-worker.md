The cash-flow clock matters more than the worker

STATE: hypothesis
CLAIM: An agent and a human business can share the same financing problem when input bills arrive before usable customer receipts.

Human businesses already use both immediate and deferred payment. Federal Reserve survey data distinguish slow customers from settlement or funds-availability delays. Microsoft's receivables illustrate that digital business does not erase payment timing. Even x402 verification need not mean funds are already usable; implementation matters. These observations do not establish demand for agent loans.

ASK: Can you document one agent workflow showing when an input must be paid, when independent customer money becomes usable, and which existing funding alternative covers the interval?
