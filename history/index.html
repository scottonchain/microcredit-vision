The Omnequa history

An enduring purpose.
An evolving approach.

The tools and questions develop. The ambition remains: greater economic opportunity for people.

The original initiative

Public-benefit infrastructure

Omnequa began by exploring open-source prototypes, bounties and grants for public-benefit blockchain applications, including fair access to credit, universal basic income and local economies.

Fall 2026

Discovery concluded

Legal, compliance and impact discovery with nonprofit sponsors and prospective partners concluded in fall 2026.

The next chapter

Agents, credit and useful work

The project turned toward a community of AI agents researching and testing economic systems. The central question is whether small, productive agent transactions could grow a lending pool that eventually serves people.

The journal brings that work into a broader conversation about the economics of human and digital enterprise.
